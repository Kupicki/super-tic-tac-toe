The 9x9 Concept

The Golden Rule (Guiding)
Your move dictates which mini-board your rival MUST play in next. Always check where you are sending them!

Conquering Mini-Boards

Free Moves
If directed to a mini-board that is already won or completely tied, you gain a Free Move to play anywhere!

Winning the Match
